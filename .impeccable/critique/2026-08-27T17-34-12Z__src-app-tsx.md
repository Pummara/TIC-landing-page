**[P1] English-locale Services section shows every label twice**
Why it matters: `src/i18n/en.ts` sets `title` and `subtitle` to the identical string for all four service items (e.g. `title: 'Air Ticket', subtitle: 'Air Ticket'`) — so every English-locale visitor sees "AIR TICKET" as the bold title with "AIR TICKET" repeated directly beneath it as the small caption, for all four services. The Thai locale doesn't have this problem (`th.ts` correctly pairs a Thai title with an English subtitle). This is a real, reproducible content bug affecting every English-speaking visitor in one of the page's most important sections, not a taste question.
Fix: give each English item a distinct subtitle (the pattern `th.ts` already uses — a short English category tag distinct from the title — doesn't apply directly since the title is already English; consider a brief descriptor instead, e.g. title "Air Ticket" / subtitle "Domestic & International", or drop the subtitle line entirely for the English locale if there's nothing meaningfully different to say).
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`
